OUR STORY

It all started in October.

Back in October, when I opened my other account and saw your ID, I sent you a text just for fun. You told me you had changed and that's how we became friends.

I never knew that one random text would become something that would mean this much to me.

♡

At one time you used to love me a lot too. I remember when you didn't have a phone, you would somehow find a way to message me at random times.

We never used to ignore each other and we always listened to each other. Your efforts at that time, trust me, were one of the best things that made me fall even more for you.

NOVEMBER

Those late nights.

When I went to Pakistan in November, we would talk until 6 AM and send each other reels. Life was so happy back then honestly.

After that we kept getting closer, but at the same time we drifted apart too. You would get upset and I would keep trying to make it up to you over and over again.

✦

We used to call each other too. When I woke up I would see your message and it would be around 12 in the morning. Then after a while you would call me and we would talk.

Then we would text and at night we would video call and just keep looking at each other. We used to watch reels together and sometimes you would even fall asleep on the call.

THE MOMENTS

Some moments stay.

Once things got so emotional that we were both crying on the call. I was asking you to stay with me and you were in the kitchen at that time because your tablet didn't have any charge.

There was one time I fell asleep without making up to you and then you saw my ID with some smoking stories on it, so you got even more mad. Then I spent all my time trying to make things right.

We met up once too, and we had such a great time together.

Look what happened in one year.

Things went wrong and right between us many times, but now things have gotten really messed up. I tried so much and I am still trying, but now it's up to you.

We made promises to each other and even if you break yours, I will still keep mine because I truly want you.

Simply talking to you made my day. You could change my whole mood just by talking to me.

♡

You used to say that I talk too much, but at one point you used to do the same thing. We used to talk so much.

MEMORIES

I remember everything.

To be honest, you look so pretty. Your eyes and that mole on your face when I saw it, oh God, mashaAllah.

And that day after the exam we were both staring at each other. I was shy but you were with your friends laughing.

Actually I love your laugh too. There are so many other things that can't just be said, but I remember everything.

JULY

Then came the distance.

When you went to Pakistan in July we didn't have any contact. I know before you went to Pakistan everything had gotten better between us, but after you went there I don't know, maybe something happened.

We didn't contact each other because of your network problem.

I would still wonder where you were going throughout the day. I used to think about you all the time.

Even while watching reels I would think about you. While watching a movie I would think about you and even when I went outside somehow you were on my mind everywhere.

OLD MESSAGES

Sometimes I look back.

I still look at our messages sometimes, listening to your old voice messages and damn, I think: where is my Fayeee.

My most favorite message was that 5 minute one, if you remember.
