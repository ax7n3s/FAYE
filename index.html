Where Everything Unexpectedly Began

You always used to talk about death and said you wanted to die. I told you I'll come to bury you and you replied that I should have some rights for that too. THE WAY WE BECAME

At one time you used to love me a lot too. I remember when you didn't have a phone you would somehow find a way to message me at random times. We never used to ignore each other and we always listened to each other.

NOVEMBER

Those Nights That Reached 6 AM

When I went to Pakistan in November we would talk until 6 AM and send each other reels. Life was so happy back then honestly. We could keep talking for hours and suddenly realise how late it had become.

After that we kept getting closer but at the same time we drifted apart too. You would get upset and I would keep trying to make it up to you over and over again. But those nights, those reels and those conversations are still some of the memories that stay with me.

OUR CALLS

From Messages To Looking At Each Other

We used to call each other too. When I woke up I would see your message and it would be around 12 in the morning. Then after a while you would call me and we would talk. Then we would text and at night we would video call and just keep looking at each other.

We used to watch reels together and sometimes you would even fall asleep on the call. Those simple calls weren't always about doing something special. Sometimes it was just you being there, and somehow that was enough.
