OUR STORY

Where it all began.

🗓️

October — Where It Started

Back in October, when I opened my other account and saw your ID, I sent you a text just for fun. You told me you had changed and that's how we became friends. You always used to talk about death and said you wanted to die. I told you I'll come to bury you and you replied that I should have some rights for that too.

Then you said you would die and I told you even if you die I still want you. After that I fell for you deeply… very deeply. What started as one random message slowly became something that I never expected to matter this much to me.

💗 ✦ 🐼 ✦ 💌
💗

The Way We Became

At one time you used to love me a lot too. I remember when you didn't have a phone you would somehow find a way to message me at random times. We never used to ignore each other and we always listened to each other.

Your efforts at that time, trust me, that's one of the biggest things that made me fall even more for you. Even when things weren't easy, somehow seeing that you still found a way to reach me meant a lot. Those little efforts became memories I still hold onto.

🌙

Those Nights

When I went to Pakistan in November we would talk until 6 AM and send each other reels. Life was so happy back then honestly. Hours would disappear without us even realizing how late it had become.

After that we kept getting closer but at the same time we drifted apart too. You would get upset and I would keep trying to make it up to you over and over again. Still, those nights, those reels, those conversations and those 6 AM moments are things I remember.

🌙 ✨ 💗 ✨ 🌙
📱

Our Calls

We used to call each other too. When I woke up I would see your message and it would be around 12 in the morning. Then after a while you would call me and we would talk.

Then we would text and at night we would video call and just keep looking at each other. We used to watch reels together and sometimes you would even fall asleep on the call. At that time talking to you had become such a normal and important part of my day.

🥺

That One Call

And once things got so emotional that we were both crying on the call. I was asking you to stay with me and you were in the kitchen at that time because your tablet didn't have any charge.

It was one of those moments where everything felt real and emotional at the same time. Out of all our calls, some moments were funny, some were peaceful and some became memories that stayed.

💭

Things We Went Through

There was one time I fell asleep without making up to you and on top of that you saw my ID which had some smoking stories on it so you got even more mad. Then I spent all my time trying to make things right.

Things went wrong and right between us many times but now things have gotten really messed up. I tried so much and I am still trying but now it's up to you.

🫶🏻

Our Meetup

We met up once too and we had such a great time together. Even though it was only a few minutes, it became one of those memories that felt special. We exchanged gifts and had that small moment together.

Sometimes a memory doesn't need hours to become important. Even a few minutes can stay with you for a very long time.

❤️

Still Trying

Things went wrong and right between us many times, but I am still here trying. We made promises to each other and even if you break yours I will still keep mine because I truly want you.

If you become mine I will give you all the happiness, do whatever you say and try my best to make you feel loved.

✨

You Changed My Days

Simply talking to you made my day and I loved talking to you. When I'm with you and talking to you, you make my day better just by talking to me.

Like bro… you can change my whole mood. You used to say that I talk too much but at one point you used to do the same thing. We used to talk so much and somehow there was always something else to say.

👀

The Little Things I Remember

Just look at what happened in one year, but I am not letting go. Though the rest is up to you. To be honest you look so pretty. Your eyes and that mole on your face when I saw it… oh God, mashaAllah.

And that day after the exam we were both staring at each other. I was shy but you were with your friends laughing. Actually, I love your laugh too.

There are so many other things that can't just be said but I remember everything. Your smile, your expressions, your laugh, your little habits and the small moments that probably seemed normal at the time.

🇵🇰

July

When you went to Pakistan in July we didn't have any contact. I know before you went to Pakistan everything had gotten better between us but after you went there idk, maybe something bad happened with you.

We didn't contact each other because of your network problem. Things became quiet, but even during that silence you were still somewhere in my thoughts.

🌙

I Still Thought About You

I would still wonder where you were going throughout the day. I used to think about you all the time. Even while watching reels I would think about you.

While watching a movie I would think about you and even when I went outside somehow you were on my mind everywhere. I couldn't forget you.

💌

Where Is My Fayeee?

I still look at our messages sometimes and listen to your old voice messages and damn… I think, where is my Fayeee?

My most favourite message was that 5 minute one, if you remember. It's strange how old messages and voices can suddenly bring back so many memories.

💗 🦋 ✨ 🌸 💌 🐼 ✨ 💗
